The current candidate uses a two-root signed sandbox and descriptor-relative operations. `native/macos-files/recovery-broker.swift:177–198` opens an interior parent, stages/journals, rechecks ROOT identities, then captures from that held parent. `:200–227` syncs directories, validates captured bytes, publishes exclusively and writes final metadata. Root identity checks do not establish the current location of the held interior parent. The required authority during rename/publication must therefore be supplied by the actual sandbox, not more pathname checks. This architecture is not disproved by the unsandboxed retained-FD host-shell counterexample: recovery opens source files at each fixed operation and performs path-sensitive namespace syscalls inside its own signed sandbox. Its exact changed two-root authority still needs qualification.
