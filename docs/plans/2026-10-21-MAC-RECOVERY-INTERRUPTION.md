- Candidate FD3 handling sets close-on-exec only. Owner authorization is supplied by the checked loader/launcher; direct candidate invocation is not proof of lease fencing. Scope owner cases to actual loader admission/ownership behavior already required by the design.

## Fsync and deterministic-window limits

An ordinary writable APFS fixture cannot force a selected `fsync` syscall to fail deterministically. Permission changes or occupying final.json demonstrate journal access/publication failure, not failed fsync. Do not use original VM media, create/mount disk images, change global settings, add interposition libraries or claim power-loss durability from SIGKILL.

If exact failed-fsync coverage or the held-descriptor relocation window cannot be reached with existing tools and the unchanged binary, record UNVERIFIED and the precise missing event. A test-only instrumented build with stop/failure hooks can diagnose control flow but has a different hash and cannot certify the production candidate. Any such source change requires a separate concrete proposal; no silent hooks or activation from synthetic checks. Source inspection alone cannot turn the current root-only recheck into a containment PASS.
